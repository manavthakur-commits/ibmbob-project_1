Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive data analytics study of a multi-branch supermarket chain operating across four Indian cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Jaipur. The dataset covers 500 sales transactions spanning January to July 2026, encompassing 20 products across 8 categories. The analysis addresses data quality validation, sales recalculation, revenue performance, customer segmentation, and actionable business recommendations.

Methodology & Project Architecture
1
Data Collection & Loading CSV dataset ingested via pandas.read_csv(). All 13 required columns validated before processing proceeds.
2
Data Cleaning & Validation Missing values audited; key identifier nulls dropped; numeric columns coerced; date column parsed to datetime; duplicates removed on Invoice ID.
3
Sales Recalculation Sales = Quantity × Unit Price recalculated for every row. Rows differing by >1% from original value flagged and corrected. Zero mismatches found.
4
Feature Engineering Derived columns added: Month, Month Name, Day of Week, Quarter — enabling temporal segmentation and trend analysis.
5
Aggregation & KPI Computation GroupBy operations on Branch, Category, Product, Payment, Gender, Customer Type. Time-series aggregations for monthly and weekly trends.
6
Visualisation & Dashboard 18 Plotly interactive charts embedded in a 7-tab Streamlit dashboard with live sidebar filters (branch, city, category, date range, gender, customer type).
7
Business Insight Generation Automated insight engine analyses aggregated results and surfaces 7+ colour-coded recommendations directly in the dashboard.
Python 3.13 Pandas 2.x NumPy 1.26 Plotly 5.x Streamlit 1.35 CSV / Flat-file data

Business Insights & Recommendations
🏆
Top Revenue Category: Beverages
Generated ₹56,108 (23% of revenue). Maintain strong stock levels and explore premium SKUs (cold brew, specialty teas) to sustain leadership.
📍
Top Branch: Branch C — Mumbai (₹72,469)
Leads in revenue and transaction count. Document its store layout, staffing, and promotional strategies and replicate across Branches A and D.
🛒
Best-Selling Product: Cheese (₹27,906)
Cheese, Coffee, and Shampoo are near-equal top-3 products contributing ~34% of total revenue. Ensure these are always in stock, especially at Branch C.
📉
Lowest Revenue Category: Bakery (₹6,516)
Despite the highest customer rating (4.24), Bakery contributes only 2.7% of revenue with just 28 transactions. Increase product range, visibility, and run combo offers to grow footfall in this segment.
🍎
Fruits Category: Low Rating Alert (3.83)
Fruits is the only category below 3.9. Investigate freshness, supplier quality, and storage conditions. A targeted quality improvement can directly boost repeat purchases.
📅
February Dip: ₹30,068 (lowest month)
February recorded 30% lower revenue than January. Investigate supply chain disruptions or seasonal demand drops. Run February-specific promotions proactively next year.
💳
UPI is the Preferred Payment Method
UPI leads with ₹67,910 and the highest avg order value (₹534.73). Ensure UPI checkout is frictionless and consider UPI-exclusive cashback offers to drive larger basket sizes.
👥
Strong Loyalty Programme — Members: 58.5% of Revenue
Members drive ₹1,43,009 (58.5%) of total revenue. Grow the membership base — even a 10% increase in Normal → Member conversion would add ~₹10,000 in incremental revenue.
📅
Peak Day: Monday | High-Value Day: Sunday
Monday generates the highest daily sales (₹42,602). Sunday customers spend the most per visit (₹601.97 avg order). Staff adequately on both days; consider targeted weekend bundles.

12
Conclusion

This project successfully demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics pipeline for supermarket sales data. Starting from raw CSV ingestion, through data cleaning, feature engineering, and aggregation, to an interactive multi-tab Streamlit dashboard with 18 Plotly visualisations — the system provides actionable visibility into every dimension of business performance.

Key findings confirm that Beverages, Personal Care, and Dairy are the revenue pillars; Branch C (Mumbai) is the performance leader; the loyalty programme is functioning well (58.5% member revenue share); and overall customer satisfaction (3.99/5) is healthy — with targeted improvement needed in Fruits and Bakery visibility.

With the reusable dashboard in place, business stakeholders can now self-serve insights, apply live filters, and download custom reports — reducing the analytics turnaround from days to seconds.
